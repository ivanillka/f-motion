# Reel standard

A quiet, readable cut. Hold the draft to these rules before you show or render
it.

## Motion

- Gentle zoom only: a scene drifts from about 1.00 to 1.06.
- Do not push past 1.06 unless the user asks for Strong.
- Keep the frame calm. No snap zooms and no fast pans.

## Picture and type

- Text is never cropped. Keep a 6% safe inset on every side.
- Spoken type is large, sentence case, and centered.
- If a line still does not fit, shorten the line or set the scene to show the
  whole picture.
- The F-Motion mark is a compact, quiet corner credit at 45 percent opacity.

## Voice and timing

- A spoken shot lasts as long as its voice take plus a short breath.
- Do not cut a take short or leave silence inside a line.
- Read the real take, not a word count.

## Preview and render

- The preview must match the export: same type, safe inset, motion, mark, and
  word highlight.
- Run `fmotion check <projectId>` after compose and before any render. Fix
  every error. Read the warnings.
- The `/v1` equivalent is `GET /v1/projects/{id}/check`.

## Paid calls

- No paid FAL or Kokoro call unless the user already confirmed the price.
- Ask first, show the quote, then wait for a clear yes.

## Scope

- Stay on the cut. No merge or deploy advice.
