Privacy notice

This is a public stub for f-motion.com while Gate 0 launch policy evidence is finalized. It summarizes how F-Motion intends to handle data in the hosted product.

What we process

  • Account identity from the configured auth provider (for example email magic link or Google, when enabled).
  • Project briefs, storyboards, and media you upload or attach for rendering.
  • Operational logs needed to run renders, diagnose failures, and secure the service.

Provider keys

When you connect Pexels or FAL, your API key is validated and stored encrypted server-side for your account. F-Motion does not display a saved key again and does not use a platform-shared Pexels or FAL key.

What we do not do (MVP)

  • No third-party marketing pixels on the marketing pages.
  • No selling of your project media.
  • No social publish from F-Motion in the current MVP.

Retention

Project and media retention follows the hosted environment’s operational needs and will be stated precisely in the Gate 0 policy package. You can request account closure via the contact below.

Contact

Privacy questions: partners@f-motion.com. Related: Terms.
