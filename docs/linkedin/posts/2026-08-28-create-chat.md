F-Motion 0.2.0 — Create is now a short chat, not a form.

You say what you want to make. The studio asks at most four missing questions,
then opens the storyboard. No second “pick a story approach” screen. Dropped

trim, level, and mute sit next to the music dock, with spoken words highlighted
on the full caption.

This is live alpha, not a commercial launch. Pexels and FAL stay your keys.
