Account Deletion Consequences

Before you proceed with your account deletion request, please review the critical implications for your Workspace and associated data.

Account Access

Immediate revocation of access to the Fotium Motion Studio and all associated API endpoints.

folder_delete

Projects & Drafts

All active projects, renders, and drafts will be queued for permanent removal from our servers.

Queued Jobs

Any currently rendering videos or AI upscaling jobs will be immediately terminated and discarded.

key_off

FAL Keys & Credits

Unused credits will be forfeited. Linked FAL API keys will be unlinked from the Fotium environment.

info Note on Data Retention: Submitting this request initiates a scheduled deletion process. While access is revoked immediately, complete erasure of data from secure backups may take up to 30 days in compliance with our retention policy.
